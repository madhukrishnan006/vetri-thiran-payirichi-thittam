Project Planning – Markdown Words

Project Planning – Deciding how the project will be completed successfully.

Objectives – The goals that the project aims to achieve.

Scope – Defines what the project includes and excludes.

Tasks – Individual activities required to complete the project.

Timeline – The schedule for completing project activities.

Milestones – Important stages or achievements in the project.

Resources – People, tools, materials, and money needed.

Budget – Estimated cost required for the project.

Team – People responsible for completing the project.

Risk Management – Identifying and managing possible problems.

Scheduling – Arranging tasks according to time and priority.

Deliverables – The final outputs or results of the project.

Monitoring – Tracking the progress of the project.

Evaluation – Checking whether the project meets its objectives.
