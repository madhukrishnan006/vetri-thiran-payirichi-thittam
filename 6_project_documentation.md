ServiceNow Workflow Studio is used to create a **“Standard laptop task”** flow with a Service Catalog trigger.  
The **Create Catalog Task** action is selected to automate laptop task creation.

The **Standard Laptop Catalog Item** is configured with the **Service Catalog Item Request** flow.  
The **Process Engine** tab connects the catalog item to the workflow automation process.
<img width="1600" height="900" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-06 at 4 27 34 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/84843f09-8751-42e2-864e-bc96488e277b" />
The ServiceNow **Service Catalog** displays different categories such as Hardware, Software, Desktops, Mobiles, and Office.  
The **Standard Laptop** request is available under the Hardware catalog for users to order a laptop.
<img width="1600" height="900" alt="WhatsApp Image 2026-10-06 at 4 27 35 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/edd9cdeb-58ec-4b05-8974-fc53346e4557" />
The **Catalog Items** list shows available ServiceNow items with details such as name, description, active status, category, price, and type.  
It allows administrators to **activate or deactivate** catalog items and manage service requests.
