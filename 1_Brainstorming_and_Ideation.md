Brainstorming and Ideation – Markdown Words

Brainstorming – Generating many ideas freely without immediate judgment.

Ideation – The process of creating, developing, and communicating new ideas.

Creativity – Producing original and useful ideas.

Innovation – Turning new ideas into useful solutions.

Mind Mapping – Organizing ideas visually around a central topic.

Problem Solving – Finding effective solutions to a problem.

Idea Generation – Creating different possible ideas or solutions.

Collaboration – Working together to develop ideas.

Critical Thinking – Analyzing ideas and selecting suitable solutions.

Divergent Thinking – Exploring many different possibilities.

Convergent Thinking – Narrowing ideas down to the most suitable option.

Prototype – Creating an early model of an idea.

Feedback – Getting opinions to improve an idea.

Concept Development – Developing a basic idea into a detailed concept.
